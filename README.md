BOROUGH PARK | OFFICIAL RULEBOOK
COMMUNITY RULES
 ROLEPLAY GUIDELINES

1. General Community Rules
1. Respect Everyone: Harassment, discrimination, racism, threats, and personal attacks are prohibited.
2. No Toxicity: Do not bring out-of-character arguments into the community.
3. No Exploiting: Abusing bugs, glitches, scripts, or unintended mechanics is prohibited.
4. No Cheating: External cheats, mod menus, injectors, and unauthorized advantages are prohibited.
5. No Ban Evasion: Using another account to avoid a punishment is prohibited.
6. No Advertising: Advertising other FiveM servers without permission is prohibited.
7. Follow Staff Instructions: Staff directions must be followed during moderation situations.
8. No False Reports: Intentionally submitting fabricated evidence or false accusations is prohibited.
2. General Roleplay Rules
1. FailRP: Actions that are unrealistic or break the roleplay environment are prohibited.
2. Metagaming (MG): Using information your character did not learn in-game is prohibited.
3. Powergaming (PG): Forcing actions or outcomes onto another player without allowing reasonable responses is prohibited.
4. Random Deathmatching (RDM): Killing or attacking someone without sufficient roleplay initiation is prohibited.
5. Vehicle Deathmatching (VDM): Using a vehicle as a weapon without appropriate roleplay context is prohibited.
6. Value of Life (VOL): Your character must react realistically to credible threats.
7. Combat Logging: Disconnecting to avoid an active roleplay situation or its consequences is prohibited.
8. New Life Rule (NLR): Follow the server's approved memory-loss rules after your character is revived or respawns.
9. Cop Baiting: Deliberately provoking law enforcement without meaningful roleplay is prohibited.
10. Scene Interference: Do not interfere with an active scene without a valid roleplay reason.
3. Character Rules
1. Characters must have believable names and backgrounds.
2. Your character must maintain a consistent personality and story.
3. Do not use another player's identity or character without permission.
4. Do not transfer information between unrelated characters.
5. Character creation must follow server naming standards.
6. Do not create characters solely to evade punishments.
7. Keep in-character and out-of-character situations separate.
4. Combat & Violence Rules
1. Violence must have reasonable roleplay initiation.
2. Players must allow scenes to develop naturally.
3. Do not attack players simply because of an out-of-character disagreement.
4. Do not exploit animations or combat mechanics.
5. Do not repeatedly target the same player without meaningful roleplay.
6. Injuries must be acknowledged and roleplayed appropriately.
7. Do not interfere with ongoing scenes simply to disrupt them.
8. Players must respect reasonable opportunities for others to respond.
5. Criminal Roleplay Rules
1. Criminal activities must have believable roleplay motivations.
2. Robberies must follow server-specific requirements and limitations.
3. Hostages must have an opportunity to participate in the scene.
4. Do not use unrealistic demands or impossible situations to force outcomes.
5. Do not exploit criminal scripts or activities.
6. Do not use outside information to locate or target players.
7. Criminal organizations must follow approved faction guidelines.
8. All criminal scenes must respect server escalation requirements.
6. Police & Emergency Services Rules
1. Law enforcement must remain professional and maintain realistic roleplay.
2. Officers must follow department policies and chain of command.
3. Do not abuse police equipment, vehicles, or department permissions.
4. Officers must have reasonable grounds for searches, arrests, and enforcement actions under server policy.
5. EMS must prioritize medical roleplay and treat scenes appropriately.
6. Do not use emergency services to gain unfair advantages.
7. Department members must not abuse confidential information.
8. Department leadership is responsible for maintaining activity and discipline.
7. Vehicle Rules
1. Drive realistically according to road conditions.
2. Do not intentionally ram vehicles without appropriate roleplay context.
3. Do not use vehicles to exploit map boundaries or mechanics.
4. Roleplay significant vehicle accidents and injuries.
5. Do not store or retrieve vehicles to evade active scenes.
6. Respect designated parking and vehicle regulations.
7. Do not use unrealistic vehicles or modifications unless permitted.
8. Safe Zone Rules
1. No initiating violence inside designated safe zones.
2. No robberies or criminal activities inside protected locations.
3. Do not use safe zones to bait or evade active roleplay.
4. Do not exploit safe-zone boundaries.
5. Staff-designated protected locations must be respected.
9. Faction & Gang Rules
1. Every faction must maintain an approved roster.
2. Factions must follow leadership and organizational requirements.
3. Members must represent their faction appropriately.
4. Faction conflicts must develop through meaningful roleplay.
5. Do not use out-of-character information in faction disputes.
6. Do not impersonate members of another faction.
7. Faction recruitment must follow server guidelines.
8. Faction leaders are responsible for member conduct.
9. Do not use faction status to intimidate players outside roleplay.
10. Faction strikes and removals may be issued for repeated violations.
10. Robbery & Hostage Rules
1. All robberies must follow the server's approved robbery limits.
2. Hostages must be given reasonable opportunities to participate.
3. Do not take hostages without a valid roleplay scenario.
4. Do not force unrealistic demands.
5. Do not interfere with an ongoing robbery without a valid reason.
6. All participants must follow applicable cooldowns and scene restrictions.
11. Staff Rules
1. Staff must remain professional and unbiased.
2. Staff must not abuse administrative permissions.
3. Staff must not favor friends or faction members.
4. Staff must maintain confidentiality.
5. Staff must provide reasonable explanations for disciplinary decisions.
6. Staff must not use administrative tools to interfere with legitimate roleplay.
7. Staff reports must be reviewed fairly.
8. Staff members must follow the same community rules as everyone else.

12. Punishment Classification
Use these classifications to keep punishments organized.
CLASS A — Minor
Low severity

Examples:
- Minor chat disruption
- First-time minor rule violations
- Disrespectful behavior
Possible punishment: Verbal warning or written warning.

CLASS B — Moderate
Medium severity

Examples:
- Repeated minor violations
- Minor FailRP
- Disruptive behavior during scenes
Possible punishment: Formal warning or temporary suspension.

CLASS C — Serious
High severity

Examples:
- Metagaming
- Powergaming
- Repeated RDM or VDM
- Exploiting server mechanics
Possible punishment: Extended suspension or temporary ban.

CLASS D — Severe
Critical severity

Examples:
- Cheating
- Ban evasion
- Serious harassment or threats
- Malicious exploitation
Possible punishment: Permanent ban or removal from the community.

Staff should consider intent, evidence, severity, history, and circumstances before assigning a punishment. These are suggested classifications, not automatic sentences.
13. Ban Appeals
1. All appeals must be submitted through the official Discord ticket system.
2. Players must provide their Discord username and character name.
3. Include the reason for the punishment.
4. Provide evidence when available.
5. Do not spam staff about pending appeals.
6. False evidence may result in further disciplinary action.
7. Management reserves the right to uphold or overturn punishments after review.
